## Panorama prompt

Create ONE continuous extremely tall portrait pixel-art background for a vertical jumping mobile game, aspect ratio 1:3 or taller, ideally 1024 by 3072. This is a single coherent landscape elevation panorama, NOT repeating tiles, NOT panels. The camera begins at the BOTTOM and moves upward along this painting. At the very bottom 15%: charming detailed Formula 1 western paddock, timber garages, tiny checkered pennants and tire stacks along the sides, dusty track at bottom edge. Middle 55%: a beautiful deep terracotta canyon with towering stratified cliff walls along left and right, distant hazy gorge and occasional small ledges, seamlessly growing upward out of the paddock rocks. Upper 30%: the cliff summits gradually disappear into cream clouds and open soft blue daytime sky. Very top 15% pure calm blue sky with only sparse clouds at edges so an infinite sky can continue above. One shared lighting direction and cohesive restrained warm-sand / terracotta / dusty sky-blue palette throughout; atmospheric perspective. Authentic polished 16-bit pixel art with deliberate square pixel clusters, designed to match a pixel cowboy racing hero. Keep central 55% quiet low-contrast open space for gameplay; scenery detailed along sides. No text, HUD, platforms, characters, frames, panels, repeated garages, duplicated horizons, upside-down scenery, neon or photorealism. Make the vertical journey physically coherent: ground ONLY at bottom, canyon cliffs in the middle, open sky ONLY above. Premium crafted game environment.
