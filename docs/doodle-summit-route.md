# Summit route and mobile fill — 2026-10-19

Mobile canvas now uniformly covers the container instead of fitting inside it.
It is bottom-aligned so shorter browser viewports keep the starting platform.
The logical world viewport remains fixed during browser zoom: extra CSS height
cannot reveal extra platforms. Excess scenery is clipped, sprites are not stretched.

Summit route repeats an 18-platform pattern: two moving cable-car roofs,
three other moving platforms, two fragile footholds, and four consecutive purple
platforms. Goat encounters are scheduled independently. Gaps start at 142–162
world units and increase to 157–177 over the first 6000 units of climb. Normal
platforms narrow from 55 to 47 units. The reachable-X calculation reserves the
full movement range of both adjacent platforms (16 units each).
Purple platforms bounce at 780
instead of 720. Goats crouch, hop, and recover; top landings bounce at 850 and stun
the goat for 1.2 seconds, side contact during the hop nudges rather than kills.
The bath commentator and both existing phrases remain available, with the admin
preview providing access to the whole summit biome. Speech is text, not audio.

Balance update — 2026-10-20: the first goat is eligible after the initial viewport
plus 3000 units of climb, with later goats separated by at least 6500–9000 units
(and delayed until a suitable platform). The first natural bath is scheduled
after the initial viewport plus 5000 units; subsequent baths require another
11000–15000 units. Goat and bath encounters cannot be scheduled within 2200
units of one another. These are climb distances, not wall-clock timers; stationary
players do not spawn more events. Admin preview still summons the bath immediately.

Summit replaces winter fish with these encounters. Assets load only with summit,
not at startup. Offscreen platform/goat work is culled, sprite raster sizes are
cached, bath text layout is cached, and winter/summit hazard clearing reuses its
array. Original hero artwork and rendering resolution are unchanged.
