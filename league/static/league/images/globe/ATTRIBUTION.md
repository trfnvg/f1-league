# Earth imagery

Earth day and night textures by Solar System Scope / INOVE, based on NASA imagery.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

earth-day.jpg: unmodified 2048×1024 day map, mirrored at
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sanghel/solar-system-3d/develop/public/textures/earth.jpg
earth-night.jpg: Wikimedia-generated 1280×640 preview of the same author's night map:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Solarsystemscope_texture_8k_earth_nightmap.jpg
https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/b3/Solarsystemscope_texture_8k_earth_nightmap.jpg/1280px-Solarsystemscope_texture_8k_earth_nightmap.jpg

Rendering changes: projection onto a sphere, dark colour grading and atmosphere
are applied at runtime. The original files are not recoloured or cropped.

Circuit coordinates: Tomislav Bacinger, MIT, same license already retained in
league/data/CIRCUITS-LICENSE.txt. Source:
https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits/blob/master/f1-locations.json
